## 1. Brief

**Claim.** Personas itself costs nothing - the app, its MIT source and every
capability, with no account or licence key. What you pay for is the model your
agents run on: your own Claude Pro or Max subscription, paid to Anthropic, never to
Personas.

**Five-second answer.** "The Personas app is free and MIT-licensed with every
feature; my agents run through Claude Code on my own Claude Pro/Max plan, so the
only bill is Anthropic's, and the app shows what each run would cost on the API."

**Overrides of the director's brief (checked in the app):**
- "free on a local model" is **not true today**: native Ollama execution is
  *DEFERRED, not a shipping feature* (`src-tauri/engine/src/ollama.rs:7-20`), and
  `cli_args.rs:17-27` says the Ollama/LiteLLM/Custom provider paths were removed. No
  variant draws a local model. The current section's "BYOM - Claude or local Ollama"
  bullet and the accepted /features AI-models router (Ollama lane) both claim it:
  finding, not fixed here.
- "Starter / Team / Builder switchable in Settings": the runtime switch was retired
  (`src/hooks/utility/interaction/useTier.ts:4-8`); the tier is fixed at build time.
  Either way not a price tier. No variant draws tiers.

**Evidence a sceptic needs.** Who is paid, for what: a $0 next to Personas with the
reason (MIT, every feature), and the one real cost named with its payee (Claude
Pro or Max -> Anthropic). Bonus evidence: the app itself shows the cost.

**Process or state?** Mostly a state (what is free, what is not). One process worth
showing: a run's path (Personas -> Claude Code CLI -> Claude) and where the money
goes (around Personas, to Anthropic).

**Audit of the current section** (1195px tall at 1366x657, 2.1 stages; `fit="min"`;
~190 words):
- would it change? No. Six feature cards with bullet lists would look the same on a
  paid product. The offer box's badges ("Free forever / Self-hosted / Open source")
  are the only pricing content, all words.
- swap test: the six cards could sit in any features section unchanged.
- caption test: "notice the six feature groups" - repeats nothing about price.
- informative: offer badges, CTA. Decorative: card glows, icons. The claim "free"
  never says what is *not* free - the reader cannot tell whether a model costs money.
- fidelity: "BYOM - Claude or local Ollama" (Ollama not shipping), "Free forever"
  heading "Everything is free" over-claims (the Claude plan is required).
- degraded: reveals are framer `fadeUp` from `SectionWrapper` (whileInView); no loops
  in the section itself (the CTA border animation is `motion-safe:` gated).

## 3. The chosen three

Why these differ: **Bill** argues *how the money moves* (a run and a payment over
time); **Spend** argues *what you will see* (the app's own cost panel); **Download**
argues *what is inside vs what plugs in* (inventory in space).

All three: `<SectionWrapper fit="fill" aria-labelledby="compare-heading">`, heading
`<h2 id="compare-heading">` via `SectionIntro` (heading "Personas is" + gradient
"free" - the current "Everything is free" over-claims, since a Claude plan is
required), art in `data-stage-slot` > `data-stage-art` > `data-illustrate-art`, the
primary CTA "Get started free" under the art with
`trackDownloadClick(DOWNLOAD_PLAN, "pricing", detectPlatformKey())`, one progress
value played once in view, replay button, `useStillMotion`, end state in SSR and
under reduced motion. Words in `const WORDS` at the top of each file.
