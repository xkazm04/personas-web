## Why this round exists

The same five sections were prototyped an hour ago under 1.1.1 and the owner
discarded all 15 variants: "huge illustrations representing very little, cutting all
text leading into no idea what is meant behind. Balancing of visual/text part was not
successful, so sizing and fidelity of the visual side." The directions that failed,
so you do not repeat them:

- team canvas: a missions list of skeleton rows; persona lanes with bare "Goal /
  QA / Landed" labels; a four-piece puzzle ("Goal" -> "Shipped").
- get started: a skeleton app window with a tour rail; five icons growing along a
  floor ("Download ... Improve"); a path inside a laptop with a struck-out cloud.
- pricing: an app window with a "$0 everything" tag; six unlabelled lanes from
  "Day 1" to "Forever"; a balance scale ("Everything" vs "$0").
- healing: a skeleton timeline; four unlabelled failure bars; a kintsugi bowl.
- lab: a version table; a staircase of generations; a balance of scenario bars.

Each was large, sparse, generic (it would fit a competitor's page) and nearly
wordless. The accepted reference sections on /features (memory run-twice, AI-model
router, security nested vault) show the bar: one specific mechanism at a size that
fits its content, every label a real product noun, a heading plus a plain lede.

## Constraints of this page (constraints, not targets)

- The page uses a desktop "stage" system (`src/styles/stage.css`): every section is
  one screen high on desktop (1366x768 laptop through 2560x1440). Build each variant
  as `<SectionWrapper fit="fill" ...>` with the current section's exact `id` /
  `aria-labelledby` props. Put the art inside an element with `data-stage-slot`: it is
  a size container that gives the art its **maximum** box. Size the art to its
  content (see the skill's size plan), centre it, leave the rest empty; never scale a
  picture up to fill the slot. Everything must fit a 1366x657 viewport under an 86px
  navbar (571px of stage) without overflow, and must not look lost at 1920x960.
- Section copy is yours to write per the skill (heading + 1-2 sentence lede). Keep
  the current heading unless your spec argues for another.
- Motion: play once when in view, replay button, `useStillMotion` from
  `@/hooks/useStillMotion`, no infinite loops, transform/opacity only, resolved end
  state in the server render and under reduced motion.
- Semantic tokens only (`BRAND_VAR`, `tint` from `@/lib/brand-theme`, `border-glass`,
  `text-foreground`, `text-muted`...). No hex, no `text-white`, no text below `/60`.
- Words in a variant live in one `const WORDS = { ... } as const` at the top of its
  file (consolidation moves the winner's copy into `src/i18n/en.ts`).
- Hooks to carry: healing keeps `data-tour-diagram="healing"` and lab keeps
  `data-tour-diagram="lab"` on the art root; get-started keeps
  `aria-labelledby="get-started-heading"` (no `id="get-started"` anywhere); pricing
  keeps `aria-labelledby="compare-heading"` and `SectionIntro id="compare-heading"` in
  `index.tsx` (src/lib/landing-address.test.ts asserts both by regex) and a working
  primary CTA ("Get started free", keep its download tracking).

## Facts the last round verified in the desktop app (C:/Users/kazda/kiro/personas)

Re-check them, and use them - they are exactly the product-specific material the
discarded variants lacked:

- **Team canvas / teams:** not a wiring canvas any more ("it's no longer a canvas" in
  `TeamCanvas.tsx`); real nouns are assignment, mission, step, Landed. The
  orchestrator runs a step graph and launches independent steps in parallel; when QA
  asks for changes the builder's step resets and is marked "round N".
- **Get started / onboarding:** the app's onboarding steps are Look & Feel, Desktop,
  Pick Template, Set Up Agent, First Run, with a left tour rail. The site's five steps
  (download & launch, connect tools, create a persona, let it work, improve) are the
  marketing framing. Cloud deployments exist (so "no cloud" is false; "no Personas
  account needed" is true).
- **Pricing:** Starter / Team / Builder are UI modes anyone can switch in Settings,
  not price tiers. Agent runs are paid to the user's own model provider (their Claude
  plan, or free on a local model); the app tracks that spend. Personas itself is free
  and MIT-licensed.
- **Healing:** `core/src/healing.rs`, `healing_orchestrator.rs`: rate limit -> wait and
  retry (backoff, at most 3 retries); timeout -> retry with a doubled limit; transient
  API error -> resume the session; credential failure -> handed to the user. No
  provider switch (failover is separate); AI healing runs only in dev mode. Labels in
  the app: "Timeline", "retry #n", "healed via retry" (`issueModel.ts`).
- **Lab:** evolution/breeding is real (`engine/evolution.rs`) but runs through Athena
  and a winner goes live only after human approval. Scoring is one composite from
  three sub-scores (not a six-axis radar). Versions & Ratings: activate a version to
  put it live, activate the previous one to roll back.
