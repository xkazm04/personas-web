## Brief

**Claim.** You get from downloading Personas to an agent that runs on its own, on
your own computer, in a handful of concrete steps - and you need your Claude Code
sign-in, not a Personas account.

**Five-second answer.** "You install Personas (it runs on your Claude Code), connect
apps like Gmail and Slack, describe an agent in a sentence, test and promote it - and
from then on it runs on its own schedule on your computer; you come back only to
improve it in the Lab."

Overrides of the round brief after checking the app: none of substance. Additions:
credentials are under "Connections" (not "Vault"/"Keys" in the sidebar), and the
build's real verbs are Describe / Answer / Review / Promote with a test before promote.

**How the site's five steps map to the app, honestly.** The site's steps (Download &
launch, Connect your tools, Create a persona, Let it work, Improve) are a marketing
framing of three different app surfaces: the first-run modal (Look & Feel, Desktop,
Pick Template, Set Up Agent, First Run), the build flow (Describe, Answer, Review,
Promote) and the day-two surfaces (triggers + Activity, Lab). "Improve" is not part of
onboarding at all - it is the Lab, later (and Team mode and up). Variant `first-run`
uses the app's own five steps; `handoff` and `setup-map` use the site's framing with
the app's real nouns at each step.

**Evidence a sceptic needs.** That the setup is a few specific actions (not a
consultancy), that nothing needs a Personas account, and that after setup the agent
keeps running without you.

**Process or state?** A process (ordered steps, then a hand-off to the agent) that
ends in a state (an agent on a trigger on your computer). Needs a time axis, a
filmstrip, or a resolved end state that shows the order.

**Audit of the current art** (`index.tsx`, `StepContent.tsx`, `visuals/*`):
- *Would it change?* No. An auto-cycling five-chip carousel with a prose card per step
  (subtitle, paragraph, three bullets) and a small visual; the picture layer is a
  platform list, six connector tiles, a prompt+persona card, an event log and a bar
  chart. None of them would differ if setup took five days.
- *Swap test:* the connector grid and bar chart would sit unchanged on the features page.
- *Caption test:* each visual's caption repeats its step title.
- Informative: the persona card (CreateVisual) is closest to real. Decorative: step
  backdrop images, the bouncing download icon, the pulse dot.
- Fidelity findings in the CURRENT section: step 3 says the agent is dropped "on your
  canvas" (the app no longer has a canvas); step 4's events (`github.prs.fetched`,
  `persona-42`) are invented and "Ten trigger types" is not what the app lists (nine
  families plus Run now); step 5's "fitness 62% -> 94%" bars are metric-shaped numbers
  with no source, and "breed ... overnight" skips that breeding runs through Athena and
  needs approval; "40+ connectors" disagrees with the app's own "200+ Built-in
  Connectors" string.
- Word count: ~120 words of prose per step inside the card.
- **Degraded paths, observed** (1366x657, dev server): the section is `fit="min"` and
  measures **768px against a 571px stage** (197px over); the carousel is gated on
  `useStillMotion` + visibility (fine); the two framer loops go through `useLoopGate`.
  Not blank under reduced motion.

**Chosen:** 1 Hand-off (mechanism), 2 First run (product-true), 3 Setup map (spatial).
The discarded directions (skeleton window with a tour rail, five icons on a floor, a
path in a laptop with a struck-out cloud) are avoided: `first-run` shows real content
(real step names, a real template, real statuses), `handoff` is a two-lane time axis
with who-does-what, and `setup-map` names every part and draws the outside services
honestly instead of striking out a cloud.

- `handoff` is not the other two: it argues with **time** (your work ends on day one;
  the agent's work continues).
- `first-run` is not the other two: it argues with **the real screen** you will see.
- `setup-map` is not the other two: it argues with **place** (what each step puts
  where on your computer, and what the agent reaches outside).

## Variant `handoff` - "Hand-off" (mechanism)

- **Five-second answer:** "I set it up once on Monday - install, connect Gmail and
  Slack, describe it, test and promote - then it runs every morning by itself; I only
  come back to put a better version live."
- **Section copy.** Heading: "From download to" + gradient "running agents". Lede:
  "You do four things once: install Personas, connect your apps, describe the agent,
  then test and promote it. After that it runs on its own schedule on your computer,
  and you come back only to improve it."
- **Label plan** (38 words):
  - lane labels: "You" (top lane), "Your agent" (bottom lane); built with 36 words
  - axis: "Mon", "Tue", "Wed", "Thu", "Fri" (day columns)
  - you-lane marks, numbered 1-4 on Monday and 5 on Thursday: "Install Personas"
    with sub-label "Claude Code signed in"; "Connect Gmail, Slack"; "Describe it in a
    sentence"; "Test & promote"; "Lab: v2 active"
  - hand-off arrow from mark 4 down to the agent lane; agent-lane chip "First run"
    with a check icon; trigger chip "Daily 08:00" at the start of the agent lane
  - agent run dots Tue-Fri with check icons (no words); dots after Thursday carry "v2"
- **Visual structure:** two horizontal lanes over a Mon-Fri axis; Monday is wide and
  dense in the top lane, the bottom lane carries a regular row of run dots. Mute test:
  the density moves from the top lane to the bottom lane - "you, then it". Facts:
  Claude Code prerequisite, Connections, the build's test + promote, schedule trigger,
  Completed runs, Lab versions.
- **Size plan:** SVG viewBox 1000 x 370, labels 15-20 units; natural size ~1030 x 400 px with the frame,
  `--art-ar` 1032/402, max-width 1060px. At the 1366x657 slot (~375px tall) labels
  render >= 13.8px. Phone: the same week on its side (time runs down), a 360 x 760 SVG.
- **Beats** (one progress value p, 3.2 s, once in view, replay button):
  1. 0.00-0.40 you-lane marks 1-4 light in order along Monday (labels are always
     present, dim until reached)
  2. 0.40-0.50 hand-off arrow draws down; "First run" lights
  3. 0.50-0.92 the agent lane's line draws Tue -> Fri, a dot lights at each 08:00
  4. 0.70 "Lab: v2 active" lights on Thursday; later dots turn to the v2 colour
- **Degraded form:** p = 1 (every mark lit) in the server render and under reduced
  motion; no loops.
- Informative: every mark. Decorative: none beyond the lane tints.

## Variant `first-run` - "First run" (product-true)

- **Five-second answer:** "After install, the app walks me through five steps - look,
  desktop apps, a template, setting up the agent - and ends by running my first agent;
  I need Claude Code, not an account."
- **Section copy.** Heading: "From download to" + gradient "running agents". Lede:
  "Install Personas and it walks you through five steps in the app, ending with your
  first agent's first run. All you need is Claude Code signed in, no Personas account."
- **Label plan** (38 words at rest):
  - window header: "Get Started"
  - step chips: "Look & Feel", "Desktop", "Pick Template", "Set Up Agent", "First Run"
  - body (the First Run step): "Execution completed successfully"; panel "Agent
    Output" with lines "Fetched unread email", "Digest sent to you"
  - filmstrip of the window's earlier screens, numbered by their step: 2 "Obsidian" +
    shield + "Approved"; 3 "Email Morning Digest" (selected template card); 4 "Email
    provider?" + "Gmail"
- **Visual structure:** one app window (the app's modal: icon badge, title, chip
  stepper with arrows, body) resting on First Run, with its three earlier screens shrunk
  beside it to what you did there. Completed chips green with a check, the current one
  violet, as in the app.
- **Size plan:** DOM, 1040 x ~320px natural (272px filmstrip + window), `data-stage-zoom`
  for tall screens; fits a 1366x657 slot (~404px) at 1x. Phone: filmstrip above the
  window, extra vertical padding so text stays under 10% of the art.
- **Beats** (4 s, once in view, replay): the filmstrip lights 2 -> 3 -> 4 while the
  matching chip is current and then ticks; First Run goes current, "Executing...", the
  output lines stream, then "Execution completed successfully" and the last chip ticks.
- **Degraded form:** the rest state above (server render, reduced motion).
- Build note: the first build swapped the window body per step; as a still that showed
  only the last step, so the earlier steps' real content moved into the filmstrip.

## Variant `setup-map` - "Setup map" (spatial)

- **Five-second answer:** "Each step puts one piece on my computer - Personas, my app
  keys in the OS keychain, the agent, its schedule and run history, the Lab - and the
  agent reaches Claude through my Claude Code and my Gmail and Slack."
- **Section copy.** Heading: "From download to" + gradient "running agents". Lede:
  "Each step puts one piece in place on your own computer. Your agent then runs there,
  reaching Claude through your Claude Code plan and the apps you connected."
- **Label plan** (~37 words): region "Your computer"; stations numbered 1-5 with a verb
  each - "Install" -> block "Personas"; "Connect" -> block "Connections" with Gmail and
  Slack icons and "OS keychain"; "Create" -> block "Email Morning Digest"; "Run" ->
  "Daily 08:00" and "Activity" with two "Completed" rows; "Improve" -> "Lab" with rows
  "v1" and "v2 Active"; outside: "Claude" with "via Claude Code", "Gmail", "Slack".
- **Visual structure:** a large rounded region (your computer, ~72% of the width) holding
  five blocks joined by a dashed order line 1 -> 5; on the right, outside the region,
  three endpoints with solid lines crossing the boundary from the agent. Mute: a local
  cluster in order + three outside endpoints.
- **Size plan:** SVG viewBox 900 x 420; natural ~930 x 450 px with the frame, `--art-ar`
  932/452, max-width 960px; labels 15-19 units. Phone: a tall SVG (360 wide) with the
  stations stacked in the region and the three endpoints below it.
- **Beats** (reveal, 3 s): stations 1-5 light in order along the dashed line; then the
  outbound lines draw. Replay button.
- **Degraded form:** all stations lit, lines drawn.
