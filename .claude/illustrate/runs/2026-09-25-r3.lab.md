# /illustrate r3 - lab (`/features`, `SectionWrapper id="lab"`), skill 1.2.0

Builder spec for the Lab section. Picture and words are one argument: the heading
states the claim, the lede says what the reader is looking at, the art names every
part with a real Lab noun.

## 1. Brief

**Claim.** You improve a persona by trying new versions of its prompt, and the Lab
proves which one is better before it goes live - with the old one a click away.

**Five-second answer** (what a cold reader should say back): "Athena drafts a new
version of the persona's prompt; the Lab scores every version 0-100 on the same test
scenarios (tool accuracy, output quality, protocol); the higher one goes live, a drop
of 5 points is flagged, and activating the old version rolls back."

**Evidence a sceptic needs:** the number that decides - one score per version, the
same test for every version, and the switch that puts the better one live and back.

**Process or state?** A process (draft -> measure -> compare -> activate / roll back)
that lands in a state (the table with one live row). Mechanism for the process,
product-true for the state.

**The real artifact:** the Versions & Ratings table (glass rows, tabular numbers, a
pulsing dot on the Active pill, emerald >= 80 / amber >= 60 / red below, a star on the
best model per version, a red warning triangle on a -5 delta).

**Audit of the current section** (4 tabs Chat / Arena / Evolution / Eval + version
rail; 1058px tall at 1366x657 = 1.85 stages; ~46-word lede plus hundreds of words of
sample chat):

- *Would it change?* Mostly no: four self-running mini-demos of prose.
- *Fidelity errors found in the app:* the Eval radar scores **six** dimensions
  (Accuracy, Clarity, Tone, Latency, Cost, Safety) - the app scores one composite of
  **three** sub-scores (F2). The Evolution tab draws a genome tree with a "breed next
  gen" button - Breed/Evolve are descoped from the UI and approval-gated (F7). The
  Arena duels two prompts for five rounds - the app's arena measures one version
  across models; versions are compared in the table (F3). The chat cites an invented
  "industry benchmark 8-12%". Versions are `v4.2`/`v4.3`; the app numbers `v4`, `v5`.
- *Caption test:* "four ways to make personas better" - restates the heading.
- Informative: the version rail (true to the app). Decorative: tab backdrops, radar,
  genome tree halo.
- Degraded: reduced motion renders all tabs resolved (useStillMotion); no blank frame.

## 3. The chosen three (default triad)

### A. `head-to-head` - "Head to head" (mechanism)

*Why not the other two:* it shows the loop in motion - where a new version comes
from, that both face the same test, and the moment the live pill moves. B shows the
screen, C shows what the number is made of.

- **Section copy.** Heading: "Every change, **measured**". Lede: "Athena drafts a new
  version of your persona's prompt, and the Lab scores it against the live one on the
  same test scenarios. You activate the winner." (Changed after the cold read: "the
  winner goes live" read as auto-promotion, which the app never does - F6/F7.)
- **Label plan** (final frame; 37 words): header chip `Inbox Triage` (the sample
  persona); left column head `v4` + pill `Live` -> `Previous` (with a rollback icon);
  right column head `v5` + pill `Athena draft` -> `Live`; centre head `Same test
  scenarios`; scenario rows `Urgent bug report`, `Newsletter says urgent`, `Meeting
  request`; totals row `Rating`, `82`, `87`, chip `+5`; beat captions `Athena drafts
  v5` / `Same scenarios, AI judge` / `87 beats 82` / `Activate v5; v4 kept for rollback`.
- **Visual structure.** A butterfly chart: a centre spine of three scenario names,
  v4's bars growing left, v5's growing right, coloured by the app's thresholds (amber
  70 -> emerald 82 on the newsletter row carries the improvement; v5 is slightly lower
  on the meeting row - an honest judge). Muted: two sides of bars on shared rows, the
  right side longer, a pill crossing from left to right. Facts: F3, F5, F6 (F2 via
  the 0-100 rating).
- **Size plan.** DOM, ~880 x 370 natural (header 44, 3 rows x 56, totals 52, beat
  strip 64, padding). Max width 900px, centred; `data-stage-zoom` scales it 1.2x on
  tall screens. Phone: the same three-column grid at 358px, beats in a 2x2 grid.
- **Beats** (mechanism; step buttons are the captions, replay button):
  1. v5 appears as `Athena draft`, empty bars.
  2. v5's bars grow row by row (v4's already there).
  3. totals `82` / `87` and `+5` appear, v5's total lit emerald.
  4. the `Live` pill slides from v4 to v5; v4 shows `Previous` + rollback icon.
- **Degraded.** Reduced motion / first paint / thumbnail: beat 4 (the finished
  comparison) with every step button clickable and instant.

### B. `ratings` - "Ratings table" (product-true)

*Why not the other two:* it is what the reader will see in the app, with its real
columns, states and the Activate button that is also the rollback - interactive.

- **Section copy.** Heading: "Only the better version **goes live**". Lede: "Every
  version of a persona is rated 0-100 on the same test scenarios, per model. Activate
  the best one; activate the previous one to roll back."
- **Label plan** (final frame; ~33 words): title `Versions & Ratings`, persona `Inbox
  Triage`; columns `Version`, `Model`, `Rating`, `Baseline`, `Status`; rows
  `v4 · Sonnet · 82 · pin + - · Measured · [Activate]`, `v5 · Sonnet · 87 star · +5 ·
  Active`, `v5 · Haiku · 79 · -3 · Measured`, `v6 · Sonnet · 76 · warning -6 ·
  Measured`. In-flight states: `Not measured`, `Measuring...`.
- **Visual structure.** A reduced Versions & Ratings table: four rows, a rating bar
  in each (threshold colours), the Active row lit emerald with a pulsing-dot pill, the
  regression row's delta red with a warning triangle, a pin on the baseline. Muted:
  rows with bars, one lit, one red. Facts: F1, F3, F4, F6 (F2 as the 0-100 rating).
- **Size plan.** DOM table, ~820 x 330 natural; max width 860px; stage zoom on tall
  screens. Phone: rating bars hidden, numbers kept.
- **Beats** (the product's own motion, no captions - the states are the captions):
  1. v5/v5/v6 `Not measured`, v4 `Active`.
  2. `Measuring...` on the three rows.
  3. ratings land (bars grow), deltas appear, v6 flagged.
  4. the Active pill moves to v5 Sonnet; v4 gets `Activate` (the rollback).
  Every `Activate` button works: the reader can roll back to v4 themselves.
- **Degraded.** The final table, fully interactive, no motion.

### C. `anatomy` - "Score anatomy" (data-as-art)

*Why not the other two:* it answers "how would I know it's better?" by opening the
number: one rating built from three judged parts, against a pinned baseline.

- **Section copy.** Heading: "Know which version is **better**". Lede: "An AI judge
  scores each version on tool accuracy, output quality and protocol, weighted into one
  0-100 rating. Pin a baseline, and a drop of 5 points is flagged."
- **Label plan** (final frame; 23 words): key `Tool accuracy 40%`, `Output quality
  40%`, `Protocol 20%`; column tops `74`, `82`, `87`, `77`; column feet `v3`, `v4`,
  `v5`, `v6`; baseline line `Baseline v4`; flag line `-5 flagged`; chips `Live` (v5),
  `Regression` (v6).
- **Visual structure.** Four columns on a 0-100 scale, each stacked from its weighted
  sub-scores (v5: 90 x 0.4 + 88 x 0.4 + 80 x 0.2 = 87), a dashed baseline at 82, a red
  line at 77, v5 crowned `Live`, v6 touching the red line. Muted: colour-stacked
  columns, one crowned, one at a red line. Facts: F2, F4, F6.
- **Size plan.** DOM chart ~720 x 360 natural (plot 260 high); max width 760px; stage
  zoom on tall screens. Phone: key below the plot, columns 56px.
- **Beats** (reveal - the last frame says everything; replay button): segments stack
  column by column (tool, quality, protocol), rating numbers appear, the baseline and
  flag lines draw, `Regression` pops on v6, `Live` lands on v5.
- **Degraded.** The finished chart.
