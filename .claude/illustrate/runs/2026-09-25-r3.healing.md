## Brief

**Claim.** When an agent's run fails for a reason that time or a second attempt fixes,
Personas works out which failure it was and retries with the fix for that failure; what a
retry cannot fix comes to you as a health issue.

**Five-second answer.** "When a run fails, Personas reads the error: a rate limit waits
30 s and retries, a timeout retries with double the time limit, a provider server error
resumes the session later. It tries up to three times; a rejected credential (or a run
still failing) is handed to me as a health issue."

**Product facts the picture must show** (verified 2026-09-25):

**Evidence a sceptic needs.** The fix differs by failure (not a generic "retry"), the
limit (three tries), and the exit: what lands on you and when.

**Process or state?** Process (fail -> classify -> fix -> retry -> healed, or hand-off).
Motion carries the fix; every variant's end frame shows the whole story.

**Real artifact.** The runner's inline HealingCard (`runner/HealingCard.tsx`: title,
severity chip, strategy line, "Retrying in 30s...", "Attempt 1 of 3") and the Health
Issues list (`issueModel.ts` states, "auto-fixed" badge).

  breaker engaged" on a rate limit (the breaker is persona-level, 5 real failures).
- *caption test*: "a connection breaks and is repaired" = the heading again.
- informative: the stage names; decorative: board, chips, particles, sparks, welds.
- degraded: under reduced motion the loop never starts - the section shows an
  all-healthy board, so the claim is never demonstrated. At 1366x657 it is 796 px in a
  571 px stage (listed in `e2e/stage-fit.spec.ts` KNOWN_OVERFLOW). 202 words, all
  hardcoded English.

## The three

Heading for all three (kept): **"Fixes itself when things"** + gradient **"break"**.

### A. `remedies` - "Remedies" / hint "Four failures, four fixes" (mechanism)

Not B or C: it is the only one that shows *how* each fix works, one shape per fix.

- **Lede:** "When a run fails, Personas reads the error and applies the matching fix, up to
  three retries. What a retry can't fix, like a rejected credential, comes to you as a
  health issue."
- **Five-second answer:** rate limit waits and retries, timeout gets double the time,
  server error resumes later, credential goes to me.
- **Label plan** (inside the art):
  - lane 1, left: `Rate limit` + code `429`; gap bracket (amber, hourglass): `wait 30s`;
    outcome: `auto-fixed`
  - lane 2, left: `Timed out`; first wall (rose): `5 min`; moved wall (amber): `10 min`;
    outcome: `auto-fixed`
  - lane 3, left: `API server error` + `529`; gap bracket (amber, play glyph):
    `resume in 10 min`; outcome: `auto-fixed`
  - lane 4, left: `Credential rejected` + `401`; hand-off chip (rose, person icon):
    `Health issue`; outcome: `for you`
  - footer key: `Up to 3 retries` (retry glyph), `then a health issue` (person glyph)
  - about 33 words, longest run 4.
- **Visual structure:** four stacked lanes; each lane = failed attempt bar ending in a
  rose cross, a fix shape, and a green retry bar - except lane 4, whose cross sends a
  dashed arrow straight to a person chip. Muted: three lanes bounce back green by three
  different shapes; one goes to a person.
- **Size plan:** HTML, all sizes in em; about 50em x 20em of content (4 lanes of 3.4em +
  footer). On the stage the art's font-size is `clamp(15px, 100cqh/21, 20px)`, so at the
  1366x657 slot (~400 px) it is ~19 px (labels 0.8em = 15 px) and at 1920x960 it caps at
  20 px (~420 px tall, ~1000 px wide) and centres. Phones: 14 px base, the lane's label
  stacks above its track.
- **Beats** (one progress value p, 4.2 s, once in view, replay button):
  1. 0.00-0.22 attempt bars grow (scaleX) in all lanes, staggered by lane
  2. 0.22-0.30 rose crosses pop at the ends
  3. 0.30-0.55 fixes: lane 1 gap bracket fades in; lane 2 the wall slides from 5 min to
     10 min; lane 3 gap bracket; lane 4 dashed arrow draws (scaleX) to the chip
  4. 0.55-0.85 retry bars grow green (lanes 1-3); lane 4 chip lights
  5. 0.85-1.00 outcome labels fade in
- **Degraded:** server render and reduced motion sit at p = 1 (everything drawn); no loop.

## Build notes (what changed against the spec, and why)

- **All three are HTML, not SVG**, sized in em. On the stage the art's font-size is
  `clamp(15px, 100cqh / emTall, 22px)` (remedies 21, run-card 22, overnight 15), so the
  art fits the slot at 1366x657 and caps at 22px on big screens; phones get 13px flow
  (labels 10.4px). Shared frame: `HealingCircuit.shell.tsx`.
- **run-card:** the log line "Reading 14 new emails" became the runner's wordless phase
  bar (RunnerPhaseTimeline), and the `medium` chip and an "Execution timed out" row were
  dropped: the variant was at 41 words / 12% text. It is now 34 words.
- **overnight:** the tags name the failure ("Rate limit · wait 30s", "Timeout · 2× limit",
  "Server error · resume in 10m"); bare 429/529 did not read cold. On phones only the fix
  shows. The schedule's pills are present from the start (neutral) and the sweep colours
  them, because the schedule exists before the night does. On phones only, the Health
  Issues slot shows the app's empty state "No open issues" until the 05:30 failure lands.
  After that it is removed, not just faded, so the end state never carries it. Play time is 3.4 s.
- Capture noise: the site picks a random theme per new browser context, and the brief's
  `--hide` selectors do not match the cookie banner (`.fixed.bottom-0.inset-x-0.z-50`).
  Phone "empty" readings moved by up to 18 points between runs for that reason.
