# Orchestration Hub
> A radial architecture-explainer: ten trigger nodes orbit a central persona hub, auto-playing through a synced trigger-detail panel - with a visible Pause/Play and Previous/Next control the visitor owns - to show how any signal can wake any agent. · **Route:** `/` (homepage section, wrapped `#pipelines`; section anchor `#orchestration-hub`) · **Status:** Live

## What it does

A homepage demo that explains Personas' trigger model in one picture. A ring of **ten trigger nodes** (Schedule, Polling, Webhook, File watcher, Clipboard, App focus, Event, Chain, Composite, Manual) orbits a central persona chip, each connected by a spoke. A continuous "signal rain" of pulses streams down every spoke toward the hub, dramatizing "any signal can wake any agent."

One trigger is **active** at a time: it glows (tinted chip, halo, brighter/faster spoke pulse) and the right-hand **Trigger detail** panel cross-fades to show that trigger's description, a concrete "fires when" condition (e.g. `POST /github/pr.opened`), the persona it wakes (e.g. *PR Reviewer*), and a deep-link into the relevant guide page. The active trigger **auto-plays** every ~9.6s, and the visitor owns it: a pill under the ring offers **Previous**, **Pause/Play** (its label says what pressing it will do) and **Next**, with the position as `n / 10`. Clicking a node, stepping, or Pause **stops** the hub on that trigger - the signal rain stops too - and nothing moves again until the visitor presses Play. Hovering or keyboard-focusing the ring, scrolling it away, or hiding the tab only **holds** it; it resumes on its own with the time it had left. Reduced-motion visitors start stopped and can reach all ten triggers with Previous/Next.

## How it works

**Playback machine** (`playback.ts`). A pure reducer (`reducePlayback`) over `{ mode, active, nextAt, remainingMs, stopped, holdUntil, focusHeld, systemHeld }`. Two kinds of pause are stored apart: the visitor's **stop** (`stopped` - Pause, `SELECT`, `NEXT`/`PREV`, or `PREFER_STILL`), which only `USER_PLAY` lifts, and the machine's **holds** (pointer, focus, system), each lifted by its own release. `mode` is derived: `stopped` wins, then `held`, else `playing`. A machine resume (held -> playing) spends the remainder banked when the hold began, floored at `MIN_RESUME_MS` (1s) so the slide never flips the instant a hold ends; a user resume starts a fresh `AUTO_CYCLE_MS` interval. A pointer hold carries `holdUntil` (`POINTER_HOLD_MS`, 30s) and lapses on the next `TICK` past it, so a touch tap with no `pointerleave` cannot wedge the cycle. Every clock-bearing action carries `now`; `nextDeadline(state)` tells the shell when a `TICK` could change anything.

**Shell** (`index.tsx`). `OrchestrationHub` holds the machine in `useReducer` (initial clock from a lazy `useState(() => Date.now())`). One effect is the only timer: it first carries a changed system hold into the machine (a `setTimeout(0)` dispatch, since the transition needs the clock), otherwise it sleeps until `nextDeadline` and dispatches `TICK`. The system hold is `useLoopGate(diagramRef)`'s `foreground`/`in-view` vetoes (tab hidden, section scrolled past); the reduced-motion preference arrives after hydration via `useStillMotion`, so a prev-state check dispatches `PREFER_STILL` when it turns on. The ring container dispatches `POINTER_ENTER/LEAVE` (non-touch pointers only) and `FOCUS_IN/OUT` (blur ignored while focus stays inside); `handleSelect(id)` dispatches `SELECT`. `mode === "stopped"` is passed to `HubRing` as `userStopped` (the loop gate's "user" decider - Pause stops the signal rain as well as the cycle) and to `TriggerDetail` as `announce`. Layout is a two-column grid (`lg:grid-cols-[1.2fr_1fr]`): the square ring container with `PlaybackControls` beneath it, and the `TriggerDetail` panel. The whole diagram carries `data-tour-diagram="orchestration"` so the guided tour can spotlight it.

**Controls** (`PlaybackControls.tsx`). A brand-tinted pill: icon-only Previous/Next (`aria-label` from `t.orchestrationHub.previousTrigger/nextTrigger`, chevrons mirrored under RTL), a Pause/Play toggle labelled with `t.tour.pause`/`t.tour.play` (the visible label changes, so no `aria-pressed`), and a tabular `n / 10` position.

**Ring geometry** (`data.ts:127-138`). `nodePosition(index, total)` places nodes on a circle: `angle = (index/total)·2π − π/2` (so node 0 sits at top), `x = CENTER + cos·RADIUS`, `y = CENTER + sin·RADIUS`, with `CENTER=260`, `RADIUS=200`, `NODE_SIZE=96`. The SVG `viewBox` is `0 0 520 520` (`HubRing.tsx:27`) — i.e. `2·CENTER` — so the ring is centered in the viewBox.

**Ring** (`HubRing.tsx`). One `<svg role="group">` rendered in z-order: (1) a slowly-rotating ambient guide circle (`rotate 360` over 80s while the loop gate runs, resting at 0 otherwise); (2) a radial-gradient hub glow tinted to the active brand via a `useId`-scoped gradient; (3) spoke `<line>`s (active spoke gets the brand color + thicker stroke); (4) the **signal-rain** `motion.circle` pulses — one per spoke, animating `cx/cy` from the node inward to `CENTER`, staggered by `i·0.3s`, with the active spoke's pulse larger/faster (`duration 1.5` vs `2.8`); (5) the central persona node (dual-theme `agents-prompts-{dark,light}.png` inside a circular `foreignObject` mask); (6) the ten `HubNode`s last so they sit on top of spokes. `useLoopGate` (`src/hooks/useLoopGate.ts`) on the svg ref yields `run` - which switches both loops' `animate`/`transition` props (via `loopTransition`), never their elements - and `still` (the motion preference), threaded to nodes as `reduced`. The `userStopped` prop feeds the gate's "user" decider; `index.tsx` sets it whenever playback is stopped.

**Node** (`HubNode.tsx`). Each node is a clickable `<g onClick>` containing: an opaque `<rect fill="var(--background)">` backing that hides the spoke line behind it, then a `foreignObject` holding a `motion.div` chip with the lucide icon + label. Active styling is data-driven inline (`tint(brand, …)` background/border, `BRAND_VAR[brand]` icon, brand glow `boxShadow`). A pulsing `motion.circle` halo renders only when `isActive && !reduced` (`HubNode.tsx:36`). Entrance spring is staggered by `index·0.05`; when `reduced`, `initial={false}` and `transition={{ duration: 0 }}` disable it.

**Detail panel** (`TriggerDetail.tsx`). Re-derives the active trigger from `activeId` (same `?? TRIGGERS[0]` fallback). An `<AnimatePresence mode="wait">` keyed on `trigger.id` cross-fades (`y: 8 → 0 → −8`, 0.3s) between: icon tile, "Trigger" eyebrow + label, description, a mono "Fires when" example, a `Bot`-iconed persona pill (always cyan-tinted, independent of the trigger's own brand), and an optional `doc` deep-link (`BookOpen` + `ArrowRight`). The card is `aria-live="polite"` only while `announce` is true (playback stopped - the visitor chose this trigger), and `"off"` during autoplay, so the cycle never talks over a screen reader.

**Node catalog** (`data.ts:35-124`). `TRIGGERS: TriggerDef[]` — ten entries, each `{ id, label, icon (lucide), brand (BrandKey, not hex), description, example, persona, doc? }`. The file comment notes it mirrors the real `personas/src/features/triggers/sub_triggers/configs`. Brands repeat across triggers (e.g. two `purple`, two `emerald`).

## Data & state
- **Source:** Static. The `TRIGGERS` array in `data.ts` is the only data source — no API, no mocks, no Supabase. Persona/example/doc strings are hardcoded literals.
- **Stores:** None (no Zustand). State is local to the section: the `useReducer` playback machine (`playback.ts`).
- **API routes:** None. The only outbound links are `<Link>`s to `/guide/triggers/*` guide pages (from each trigger's optional `doc.href`).
- **Types:** `TriggerDef`, `DocRef` (`data.ts:19-33`); `BrandKey` (`src/lib/brand-theme.ts`); `LucideIcon` (lucide-react).

## Integration points
- **Brand theming:** `BRAND_VAR` and `tint` from `src/lib/brand-theme.ts` color every node, spoke, halo, glow, and the detail panel (data-driven → inline styles, not Tailwind tokens). Triggers reference brand *keys* so they adapt to light/dark themes.
- **Motion:** `useLoopGate` + `useStillMotion` feed the playback machine's system hold and reduced-motion stop; `HubRing` reads `useLoopGate` (`src/hooks/useLoopGate.ts`) for the continuous SVG motion. `fadeUp` (`src/lib/animations.ts`) + `SectionWrapper` provide the section's scroll-reveal.
- **Layout primitives:** `SectionWrapper`, `SectionIntro` (`@/components/primitives`), `EYEBROW` (`src/lib/typography.ts`).
- **Guided tour:** `data-tour-diagram="orchestration"` is the spotlight hook for the Athena tour (see [Guided tour](../marketing/guided-tour.md)).
- **Lazy mount:** imported via `createLazySection` in `lazy.tsx` and gated behind `LazyMount minHeight={640}` so its chunk loads on scroll.
- **Central image asset:** `/imgs/guide/agents-prompts-{dark,light}.png` (decorative, `alt=""`).

## Conventions & gotchas
- **i18n gap (real issue):** the entire section renders **hardcoded English** — the `SectionIntro` heading/description (`index.tsx:47-52`), every `TriggerDef` `label`/`description`/`example`/`persona`/`doc.label` (`data.ts`), and the panel's static labels ("Trigger", "Fires when", the `→` eyebrow) in `TriggerDetail.tsx`. None route through `useTranslation()`, so this violates the repo's "every user-facing string lives in `en.ts`" rule and ships untranslated in the other 13 locales. Any copy edit here should be an i18n migration first.
- **Accessibility:** nodes are `role="button"` `<g>`s with `tabIndex`, Enter/Space handlers and `aria-pressed` (`HubNode.tsx`) inside a `role="group"` svg. Because a selection now sticks, `aria-pressed` no longer flips under a focused node. The section heading/description and trigger copy are still hardcoded English (see the i18n gap above); the playback control is translated.
- **Animation gating goes through the loop gate:** the ambient rotation and signal-rain pulses read `run` from `useLoopGate`, so they stop for reduced motion, a hidden tab, a scrolled-past section, or a user stop - previously they honoured framer's (SSR-unsafe, non-live) reduced flag only and kept animating `cx/cy` off-screen. Both elements always render; only their `animate`/`transition` props switch. The node halo and entrance spring are gated in `HubNode.tsx` on `still`; the playback machine starts stopped under `prefers-reduced-motion` (and stops when the preference arrives after hydration). `src/lib/motion/loop-gate.test.ts` fails if a framer `useReducedMotion`, a bare `repeat: Infinity` or a private IntersectionObserver reappears in this directory. Follow this pattern — `custom-animation/require-animation-gating` only catches `requestAnimationFrame`, so framer loops like these would otherwise slip past lint.
- **`view-box` transform origin:** SVG rotations/scales set `transformBox: "view-box"` + an explicit pixel `transformOrigin` (`HubRing.tsx:57`, `HubNode.tsx:47`). This is the repo's required pattern for SVG motion — omitting it makes elements orbit the wrong point.
- **Legacy wrapper id:** the section is mounted inside `<div id="pipelines">` (`page.tsx:53`, `lazy.tsx` array) — a leftover from the old "Pipelines" concept this radial hub replaced (see the comment at `index.tsx:13-17`). The `SectionWrapper`'s own id is `orchestration-hub`. Deep-links / the scroll map may use either; don't assume they match.
- **A selection sticks - the timed tap-pause was reversed on purpose (2026-09-23, owner-approved):** clicking a node, stepping, or Pause stops the hub until Play; it used to `pauseFor(AUTO_CYCLE_MS·2)` and resume after 19.2s. That timer was the workaround for having no visible control (touch taps emit no `pointerleave`, so a permanent hover-style pause could wedge). With a visible, operable Pause/Play, WCAG 2.2.2 (Pause, Stop, Hide) favours the stop the visitor chose: moving or auto-updating content that lasts over 5s needs a mechanism to stop it, and a timer must not undo that choice. The touch wedge is answered separately: touch pointers take no hover hold, and a pointer hold self-expires (`POINTER_HOLD_MS`). This includes the **Athena tour** - its orchestration step clicks schedule / event / polling / webhook through `data-trigger-id`, so after it the hub stays stopped on Webhook until Play. Do not re-add a timed resume after a selection.
- **Two fallbacks must stay in sync:** both `index.tsx` and `TriggerDetail.tsx` independently resolve the active trigger with `?? TRIGGERS[0]`; the panel re-finds by `id` rather than receiving the object. Keep `TRIGGERS` ids stable.
- **Persona pill brand is fixed:** the detail panel's persona pill is always cyan-tinted (`TriggerDetail.tsx:82-86`), regardless of the trigger's own `brand` — intentional, not a bug to "fix."
- **Geometry vs. clipping:** with `RADIUS=200`, `CENTER=260` and `NODE_SIZE=96`, node chips extend to ~308px from center — just inside the 520 viewBox, but the outer card uses `overflow-hidden` (`index.tsx:56`) as a safety clip. Changing `RADIUS`/`NODE_SIZE` without re-checking the viewBox can clip nodes.
