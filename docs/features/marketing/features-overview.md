# Features Overview
> The value-proposition story — the `/features` "Murmuration" swarm hero, a 3-card "build & operate" feature cluster and the six-layer platform stack (vision grid) · **Route:** `/features` (hero) and `/` (cluster, vision grid) · **Status:** Live

## What it does
Tells a prospect *what the platform offers* and *why agents beat rigid workflows*, in a hero plus two reusable marketing sections:

- **`/features` hero — "Murmuration"** (`feature-sections/murmuration-hero`) — the first thing on the `/features` page: a full-viewport canvas swarm of AI agents drifting in five team clouds, headline "A crew of AI agents, **alive on your machine.**" with the subhead "They wake on events, team up, and get it done. Privately.". Every arriving event ("New email", "Invoice in", "PR opened", ...) pulls the nearest free team into a working ring that flashes "Handled" and lets go; a coach light wanders between teams. The visitor can pull agents in with the pointer, press anywhere to drop "Your event", or use the **Send an event** button from the keyboard. It opens with ~3 s of the swarm assembling the honeycomb mark and bursting into teams, skippable with **Skip intro**. Calls to action are **Download** and **View on GitHub** only; there is no tour launcher because the features tour is launched from the Design section.

- **Features cluster** (`sections/features`) — a bridge intro ("you've seen *why* agents win, here's *how*"), then three numbered feature cards: design with natural language, agents that coordinate, and full visibility. One hero card (01) plus a 2-up grid (02–03); the cloud deploy card was removed (owner ruling 2026-09-14: no cloud deployment), each card carrying an inline mini-visual and two deep links into the in-product guide.
- **Vision grid — "Layer stack"** (`sections/vision-grid`) — "The platform behind your agents": one sample agent card on top of an exploded isometric stack of the six platform layers (Orchestration, BYOM, Templates, Monitoring, Lab, Credential vault). Each layer is labelled with its one-line job; selecting one pulls its slab out, lights the part of the agent card it is responsible for, and shows a detail panel (the question it answers, what it does for this agent, description, detail list, guide deep-link, and a "next layer down" step).

All three are presentational marketing surfaces: static content, no data fetching, no stores.

**Important — what actually lives on `/features`:** the `/features` *route page* (`src/app/features/page.tsx`) renders the Murmuration hero (inside `<div id="overview">`, `page.tsx:64`) but does **not** render the features cluster or vision grid documented below. After the hero it composes the long-form `feature-sections/*` deep-dives (DesignEngine, MemoryLayers, HealingCircuit, SecurityVault, MultiProviderAI, ObservabilityDeck, Lab, Plugins). Its scroll map gained `OVERVIEW` (`href: "#overview"`, `page.tsx:43`) as the first dot. The `sections/features` cluster and `sections/vision-grid` documented here are mounted on the **landing page** (`/`, the vision grid at `wrapperId: "vision"` via `sections/lazy.tsx:117`) and on the dev-only `/preview/features` and `/preview/vision` surfaces (`features` is a preview extra, `src/app/preview/registry.ts:39`; `vision` is derived from the live `LazyVision` export). The `sections/features` cluster is currently registered for preview only — it is not in the live landing `sections[]` array (`src/app/page.tsx:51-62`). Treat "Features Overview" as the value-prop *component family*, not a 1:1 mapping to the `/features` URL.

## How it works
**Murmuration hero** (`feature-sections/murmuration-hero/`). `index.tsx` (`MurmurationHero`, `"use client"`, eager above the fold) reads `landingSectionsCopy.featuresHero` and wraps everything in `HeroShell` with `className="-mt-24 stage:-mt-(--nav-h)"`: a negative margin that cancels the `InfoPageLayout` navbar spacer (`h-24 stage:h-(--nav-h)`, `InfoPageLayout.tsx:47`) so the hero owns exactly one viewport. `HeroShell` (`shared/HeroShell.tsx`) is the `data-stage-hero` section registered with the animation-pause registry (`useAnimationPauseRegister`, `suppressHydrationWarning` for the post-hydration class). The headline sits in the left column behind a radial scrim (badge `t.hero.badge` with `HoneycombMark`, h1 `id="features-hero-heading"`, subhead, `HeroCtas tour={false}`); the swarm lives on the right ~60% of the stage.

**Swarm layer** (`MurmurationArt.tsx`, `useSwarm.ts`, `swarm.ts`, `swarm-draw.ts`). `MurmurationArt` is a `role="img"` full-bleed `<canvas>` (`aria-label = copy.aria`) with pointer handlers (move draws agents in, skipped when still; press sends "Your event" at that point) and two real buttons bottom-right: **Skip intro** (while `introOn`) and **Send an event**. `swarm.ts` is the pure, frame-rate-independent simulation: `TEAM_COUNT` 5 team clouds with homes as viewport fractions, a mission timeline (`GATHER` 0.9 s, `WORK_END` 2.8 s, `RELEASE` 3.5 s), an `INTRO` of 3.2 s (agents assemble into the honeycomb mark, `markPoint`, then burst), seeded RNG (`mulberry32`). `swarm-draw.ts` paints it: `readPalette` resolves the brand colours and theme from CSS (canvas cannot read vars) and one cached glow sprite per colour is stamped per agent. `useSwarm` owns canvas sizing (DPR capped at 2, `ResizeObserver`), re-reads the palette on `data-theme`/`class` changes (`MutationObserver`), and runs the rAF loop only while `useIsVisible(wrapRef)` and motion is allowed. Under reduced motion it skips the intro and paints one composed still frame with two teams at work, and `send()` jumps straight to the gathered state.

**CTAs** (`shared/HeroCtas.tsx`) are a copy of the home hero's, with download placement `"features-hero"` (`trackDownloadClick(DOWNLOAD_PLAN, "features-hero", detectPlatformKey())`) and a `tour` prop (here `false`); with it on, it would render `TourLauncher tourId="home"`.

**Features cluster** (`sections/features/index.tsx`). A `SectionWrapper#features` runs a one-shot reveal; `FeatureBridge` self-drives its own `whileInView` stagger (it is independent of the wrapper). The `features` array (`data.ts`) is the single source of truth: index 0 is the hero card (uses `heroSlideIn`), indices 1–2 render in a `md:grid-cols-2` grid. Each grid card's entrance animation is chosen by its `entrance` discriminator (`"slideLeft" | "fadeUp" | "slideRight"`) keyed into `gridCardVariants` — decoupled from array position so reordering cards never silently re-shuffles entrances. `FeatureCardHeader` renders the accent-tinted icon chip (`ACCENT_ICON_CLASSES[accent]`), title, and `proof` caption. Per-card mini-visuals are resolved from `FEATURE_VISUALS_BY_KEY[visualKey]` (CSS/SVG mocks in `components/visuals.tsx`, each with a descriptive `role="img"`/`aria-label`). `ProgressionThread` draws a dashed vertical spine with numbered dots aligned beside each card (md+ only, `pointer-events-none`). `GuideLinks` renders the per-card guide deep-links (row layout on the hero, stacked in the grid).

**Vision grid** (`sections/vision-grid/index.tsx`, `VisionGridLayerStack`). `localizeStackLayers(visionStackCopy)` (`layer-stack/layers.ts:66`) joins the six `PLATFORM_CARDS` (`data.ts`: id, brand, guide topics) with their words from `visionStackCopy.layers[id]` into `StackLayer`s, ordered top→bottom by the `spec` table (`layers.ts:56-63`, which also maps each layer to the persona-card `part` it lights). A 500px-wide illustration box (`h-[570px]`/`sm:h-[592px]`, `index.tsx:82`) holds `LayerStackPersonaCard` (the sample agent, `PersonaCard.tsx`), `LayerSlabs` (isometric slabs drawn with a CSS `matrix`, `LayerPlates.tsx`) and `LayerLabels` — a vertical `role="tablist"` of callouts aligned to the slabs, with arrow/Home/End keys (`LayerLabels.tsx:31-43`). Beside it, `LayerDetail` is the `role="tabpanel"`. The only state is `active` (index). Motion is one reveal: the server/resting state is the exploded stack; on the client, only if the box mounts below the fold with motion allowed, the slabs are set `collapsed` and spread once when 35% in view (`index.tsx:43-58`); selection springs are zeroed when `still`.

**Stage fit.** `SectionWrapper fit="min"` and the diagram block carries `data-stage-fixed` (`index.tsx:64,75`; `src/styles/stage.css`): on the desktop stage the fixed-pixel stack and its detail panel zoom as a whole — 0.68 / 0.78 / 0.87 on short laptop screens, 1 at 1080p, 1.15 / 1.35 on tall monitors. The intro is the shared `SectionIntro`, so it takes the stage heading scale and gap.

Guide links (both sections) route through `openGuideLink(guideHref(ref))` (`@/lib/guide-link`): `<button>` not `<a>`, so the desktop app can intercept via a `personas:open-external` CustomEvent; the web build falls back to `window.open(_, "_blank", "noopener,noreferrer")`. URLs are `/guide/{category}/{topic}`.

## Data & state
- **Source:** static module constants — `features[]` (`sections/features/data.ts`) and `PLATFORM_CARDS[]` (`sections/vision-grid/data.ts`) plus the `visionStack` i18n namespace for every vision-grid word. No runtime fetch. **Stores:** none (no Zustand). **API routes:** none. **Types:** `Feature`/`FeatureEntrance`/`FeatureVisualKey` (`features/types.ts` + `components/visuals.tsx`); `PlatformCard`/`PlatformCardId` (`vision-grid/data.ts`), `StackLayer`/`LocalizedCard`/`CardPart` (`vision-grid/layer-stack/layers.ts`); shared `GuideTopicRef` (`@/lib/guide-link`); `BrandAccent`/`BrandKey` (`@/lib/brand-theme`). Only ephemeral UI state is the vision grid's `active` layer index.

## Integration points
- **Hero copy:** `landingSectionsCopy.featuresHero` (`line1`, `line2`, `sub`, `aria`, `events[]`, `handled`, `yourEvent`, `sendEvent`, `skipIntro`) plus `t.hero.badge` / `downloadCta` / `viewOnGithub`; `landingSections` is in `src/i18n/pending/` (PLAN M22) (English only).
- **`InfoPageLayout`** — supplies the navbar spacer the hero cancels, and the scroll map (`OVERVIEW` first); the features tour is launched from `DesignEngine`'s intro, which is why the hero has no launcher.
- **Guide system** — both sections deep-link into `/guide/{category}/{topic}` via `openGuideLink(guideHref(ref))`; desktop app intercepts with the `personas:open-external` CustomEvent.
- **Brand theme** (`@/lib/brand-theme`) — features use `ACCENT_ICON_CLASSES[accent]` for icon chips; the layer stack uses `BRAND_VAR[brand]` (CSS var color) and `tint(brand, n)` for slabs, callouts, lit card parts and the detail panel.
- **Landing composition** — vision grid mounts via `LazyVision` (`sections/lazy.tsx:117`) in the home `sections[]` array (`src/app/page.tsx:58`, `wrapperId: "vision"`, not gated); carries `data-tour-diagram="platform"` for the product tour, which steps through the layers by each tab's `data-card-id`.
- **Preview surface** — both registered at `/preview/{vision,features}` (dev-only, 404 in prod) via `src/app/preview/registry.ts`.
- **Illustrations** — `PLATFORM_CARDS[].images` still names `/public/imgs/platform/{id}-{dark,light}.png`, but the layer stack does not render them (no current consumer).
- **Shared primitives** — `SectionWrapper`, `SectionIntro`, `SectionHeading`, `GlowCard`, `GradientText`, animation tokens from `@/lib/animations`.

## Conventions & gotchas
- **Hero added 2026-10-05** as the runner-up of the landing review (the home page got "Hive"; see [Homepage & Hero](homepage-hero.md)). Before it, `/features` opened straight on the Design section. The `-mt-24 stage:-mt-(--nav-h)` on `HeroShell` is coupled to the `InfoPageLayout` spacer: change one, change the other, or the hero stops being exactly one viewport.
- **Hero motion gating:** the canvas loop uses `useStillMotion` and `useIsVisible` (the `require-animation-gating` lint rule sees the rAF); it never runs off-screen or under reduced motion, (the Skip button renders only while `introOn && !reduced`). Keep keyboard parity: **Send an event** is the non-pointer path.
- **i18n — split state.** The **vision grid** routes every word (heading, layer titles/descriptions/details/questions/jobs, persona card, `aria-label`s via `fillTemplate` from `@/lib/fillTemplate`) through `visionStackCopy`, which is pending modules in `src/i18n/pending/` in `src/i18n/pending/visionStack.ts` — English-only for now; the 13 other locales may omit it and the runtime falls back to English. Left in code: brand/model/connector names and sample values (`Claude`, `$0.04`, `prompt v3`). The **features cluster** is still hardcoded English in JSX (no `useTranslation`): the `features[]` copy, the bridge paragraph, headings, `aria-label`s and visual descriptions. Do not add *more* hardcoded strings.
- **Animation gating is partial.** `sections/features/index.tsx` gates its `whileHover` scale/glow with framer's `useReducedMotion`, but the cluster's CSS motion (`animate-bar-grow`, `animate-glow-border`, `FeatureBridge`'s `animate-bounce`) is ungated, and the lint rule (which only flags `requestAnimationFrame`) won't catch it. The vision grid is gated: `useStillMotion` keeps the exploded resting state and zeroes the slab springs; the collapse→explode reveal is skipped under `prefers-reduced-motion`, and the DOM is the same on server and client.
- **ProgressionThread is hand-tuned to layout.** `HERO_DOT_TOP_PX`/`GRID_DOT_FIRST_OFFSET_PX`/`GRID_DOT_SPACING_PX` (`ProgressionThread.tsx:21-23`) are pixel offsets coupled to the hero/grid card padding in `index.tsx`. Change card spacing → re-tune these or dots drift off their cards.
- **`/features` route ≠ these components.** Re-stating because it bites: editing `sections/features` or `sections/vision-grid` changes the landing page and preview, not the `/features` URL (which renders `feature-sections/*`).
- **Entrance is decoupled from order — keep it that way.** Add a 5th feature card with an `entrance` key (not a parallel array). The hero card (index 0) ignores `entrance` and always uses `heroSlideIn`.
- **Data files stay JSX-free.** `features/data.ts` references visuals by string `visualKey`; the JSX lookup lives in `components/visuals.tsx`. Don't inline components into `data.ts`.
- **Layer stack is a tabs pattern.** `LayerLabels` is a vertical `role="tablist"` (roving `tabIndex`, arrow/Home/End) whose tabs also stretch over their slabs, so clicking a slab selects it; `LayerDetail` is the `role="tabpanel"` labelled by the active tab's id. The tour selects layers by `data-card-id` (`src/lib/tour-script.ts`) — keep card ids stable.
- **Stack geometry is shared constants.** Slab positions and callout alignment both derive from `STACK` / `slabMidY` / `LABELS_LEFT` in `LayerPlates.tsx` and the `--s` CSS variable set on the box; change them together.
- **Semantic tokens, with documented exceptions.** Sections use `text-muted`, `text-muted-dark`, `border-glass(-hover)`, `text-brand-cyan`, `var(--background)` etc. Brand-colored accents are intentionally driven by `BRAND_VAR`/`tint` inline styles rather than utility classes (per the established SVG-motion brand pattern).
